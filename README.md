# Sales-Revenue-Analysis-Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales, revenue trends, top-performing products, categories, and regions.

## Overview

This project is a one-page interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze sales and revenue performance and generate business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales
- Track monthly sales trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales across product categories
- Compare sales across regions
- Use interactive slicers for analysis

## Dataset

The original dataset was obtained from Kaggle in CSV format. The data was cleaned and prepared in Excel before being used for the Power BI dashboard.

The dataset contains:

- Product_ID
- Sale_Date
- Sales_Amount
- Quantity_Sold
- Product_Category
- Region
- Customer_Type

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold

### Visualizations

- Monthly Sales/Revenue Trend
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Region

### Slicers

- Year
- Region
- Product Category
- Customer Type

## Business Insights

- January 2024 sales decreased by approximately 96% compared with January 2023.
- Product 1099 was the top-performing product overall based on total sales.
- North recorded the highest sales for Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture, while East recorded the highest sales for Food.
- Product 1089 had the highest sales in North, 1096 in South, 1033 in East, and 1001 in West.
